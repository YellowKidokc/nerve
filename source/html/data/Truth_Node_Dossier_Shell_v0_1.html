The equation describes how the chi-field is proposed to change complexity over time.

Source

“dK/dt = -alpha chi(t); alpha > 0”

Source interpretation: complexity decreases under chi-field influence.

What it says

The rate of change of complexity equals the negative product of alpha and chi.

That only implies decreasing K when both alpha and chi are positive.

dK/dt = -alpha chi(t)
What would break it
K increases while alpha > 0 and independently measured chi > 0; or alpha measures as zero.
