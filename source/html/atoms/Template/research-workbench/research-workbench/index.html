03

Evidence balance

Manual, experimental rating. Not a probability of truth. Repeated reports of one finding share a family; genuinely separate findings get separate families.

Scoring rules & sensitivity

Each family contributes its strongest assessed item in each direction. Within a cluster, sorted family strengths receive these marginal weights: 1, 0.5, 0.5, 1. At strength 5, the cumulative scores are 5, 7.5, 10, 15. Two-family value 7.5 is provisional. Later families add no points, but remain counted.

Strength rubric: 1 = weak or indirect; 2 = limited; 3 = moderate; 4 = strong; 5 = decisive within the stated scope. Explain every rating. Model-generated or repeated text is not automatically independent evidence.
