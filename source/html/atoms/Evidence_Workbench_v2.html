Evidence balance

Scoring rules and robustness

Each family contributes its strongest assessed item per direction. Within a cluster, families are sorted by discriminating strength and receive marginal weights in order; the schedule below is your proposed rule (5 / 7.5 / 10 / 15 at strength 5) and is preserved as stated. Discriminating strength = raw strength × rival factor × timing factor. Balance = support − (counter multiplier × counterevidence). A live decisive counterexample gates the display regardless of balance.

Strength: 1 weak/indirect · 2 limited · 3 moderate · 4 strong · 5 decisive within stated scope. Two items are one family if they cannot fail independently (same dataset, instrument, source document, or reanalysis). A meta-analysis is one family.
