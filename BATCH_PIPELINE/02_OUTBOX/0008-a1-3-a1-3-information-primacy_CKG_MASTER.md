### Terms and plain-language bridge
*   **Ontologically Primitive:** A fundamental building block of reality that is not composed of or reducible to anything more basic. *Analogy:* In a Lego set, the individual bricks are primitive; the models you build from them are emergent.
*   **Distinguishability:** The property that allows one state, object, or event to be told apart from another. *Analogy:* The difference between a lit bulb (1) and an unlit bulb (0) is distinguishable; that difference *is* information.
*   **`I ≡ Distinguishability`:** The symbol "≡" means "is defined as" or "is identical to." This asserts that "Information" and "Distinguishability" are two names for the same concept.
*   **`H(X) = -Σp·log p` (Shannon Entropy):** A mathematical measure of the average "surprise" or uncertainty in a set of possible outcomes. *Analogy:* The more equally likely and numerous the possible answers to a question are, the higher the entropy (more information is gained when the answer is revealed).
