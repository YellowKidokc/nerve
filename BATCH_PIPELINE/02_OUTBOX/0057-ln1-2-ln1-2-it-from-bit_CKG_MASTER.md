## Strongest objection and negative controls
**Objection:** The claim confuses epistemology with ontology. We *describe* physics using information, but that doesn't mean physics *is made of* information. Physical states (e.g., a rock's mass) exist independently of any observer making a distinction. The "hard problem" is explaining how subjective information arises from objective physical processes, not the reverse.
**Counterexamples:** 1) Quantum decoherence: The transition from quantum information (superposition) to classical physical reality is not fully understood as an informational process. 2) Phenomenal consciousness: The qualitative feel of experience ("qualia") resists purely informational description.
**Competing Interpretations:** Physicalism (physics is fundamental), Neutral Monism (both physics and information emerge from a neutral substrate), or Idealism (mind/consciousness is fundamental).
**False Conclusion Prevention:** One must not conflate the framework's internal logical necessity with an established empirical fact. The claim does not provide a testable mechanism for emergence.
