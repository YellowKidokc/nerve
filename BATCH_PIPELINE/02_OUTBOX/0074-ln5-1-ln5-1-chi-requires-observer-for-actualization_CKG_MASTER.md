## Strongest objection and negative controls
**Objection:** The necessity claim commits the fallacy of composition. Quantum systems (e.g., electrons) may require observation for definite properties, but it does not logically follow that the *substrate* from which those systems emerge has the same requirement. The χ-field could be self-actualizing or follow different, non-observational dynamics.
**Counterexamples:** 1) Decoherence theory explains definite outcomes without a conscious observer. 2) In many interpretations (e.g., Bohmian mechanics, objective collapse), definite states exist prior to or independent of observation.
**Competing Interpretations:** The axiom assumes a Copenhagen-like, observer-centric metaphysics. Competing interpretations (Many-Worlds, Realism) deny the special role of observation in actualization.
**Negative Control Test:** If a model can be constructed where `Actual(χ)` and `¬∃O: Observes(O, χ)` are both true without contradiction, the necessity is defeated.
