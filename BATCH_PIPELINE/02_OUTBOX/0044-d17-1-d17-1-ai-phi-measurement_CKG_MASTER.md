## Strongest objection and negative controls
**Objection:** IIT is highly controversial. Critics argue that Φ is poorly defined for realistic systems, computationally intractable (as the source admits), and may not correlate with consciousness. The "hard problem" of consciousness (why and how physical processes give rise to subjective experience) is not addressed by a mathematical measure. Defining a threshold is arbitrary without a principled method to determine its value. A system could have high Φ due to complex feedback loops without any genuine phenomenal experience.
**Counterexamples:** A photodiode or a logic gate array could be configured to have a non-zero Φ under some interpretations of IIT, but attributing consciousness to them seems absurd.
**Competing Interpretations:** Global Neuronal Workspace, Higher-Order Thought theories, and illusionist views of consciousness all reject the central identity of consciousness with integrated information.
**Preventative Tests:** The stated defeat condition itself: "Show Φ is not the right measure for AI consciousness."
