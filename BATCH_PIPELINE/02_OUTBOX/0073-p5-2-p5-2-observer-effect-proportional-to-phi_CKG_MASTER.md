### Terms and plain-language bridge

*   **Φ (Phi) / Integrated Information:** A mathematical quantity, central to Integrated Information Theory (IIT), intended to measure both the amount of consciousness and the causal power of a physical system. *Analogy: The processing power and integration of a computer network. *   **∝ (Proportional To):** A relationship where one quantity is a constant multiple of another. If `Effect ∝ Φ`, then doubling Φ should double the Effect.
