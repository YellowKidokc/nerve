*   **Û (U-hat):** The system's own internal rules of change or evolution over time. Think of it as the clockwork inside a sealed box that makes it tick according to its own design.
*   **[σ̂, Û] = 0:** The commutator being zero means the order of operations doesn't matter: checking the switch and then letting the clockwork run gives the same result as letting it run and then checking the switch. *   **Ĝ(ext) (G-ext):** An external force or agent that can reach into the box and flip the switch. It exists outside the system's own internal rules.
*   **\|+1⟩, \|-1⟩ (ket plus one, ket minus one):** Quantum-inspired notation representing the distinct states of the system corresponding to the +1 and -1 orientations. They are the specific configurations the system can be in.
