### Terms and plain-language bridge

* **Eigenstate (\( |n\rangle \))**: A special quantum state associated with a definite value (eigenvalue) for a specific measurement. *Analogy*: A perfectly tuned radio station frequency; the radio (measurement) tuned to that frequency receives only that clear signal.
* **Outer Product (\( |n\rangle\langle n| \))**: A mathematical operation combining a column vector (\( |n\rangle \)) and a row vector (\( \langle n| \)) to produce a matrix (the operator). *Analogy*: Combining a mold and a stamp to create a complete sealing tool.
